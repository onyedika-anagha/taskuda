Taskuda is a free day planner for people with more than one job.

Taskuda lays out each day as sticky notes in Morning, Afternoon and Evening, colour-coded by employer or client. At the end of the day it walks you through what's unfinished, carries it over, and gives you an end-of-day report ready to paste into Slack, Teams, Jira or email. It's free, works offline, and keeps your tasks on your device.

Last updated October 22, 2026

Who Taskuda is for

Taskuda is built for people who work for several employers or clients at the same time: freelancers, contractors, and people holding two part-time jobs. They often send a separate end-of-day report to each manager, and their tasks live in different places, so unfinished work gets dropped without anyone noticing. General to-do apps don't know which job a task belongs to, and team project tools are too heavy for one person.

How Taskuda works

  1. Add your companies. Each employer or client gets a name and a colour.
  2. Plan the day as sticky notes. Put each task in Morning, Afternoon or Evening, with an optional start time, duration and due date.
  3. Work through it. Move each task from To Do to In Progress to Done. Spoken reminders tell you when a timed task is about to start, and notes are marked Running late, Overran, Overdue or Stuck when work slips.
  4. Close out the day. Decide what happens to each unfinished task (carry it over or delete it), rate the day, and add a short reflection.
  5. Copy your report. One click copies an end-of-day report for all your companies or just one, formatted for Slack, Teams, Jira or email.

What's included

  • Colour-coded sticky notes for each company, with drag-and-drop and full keyboard support
  • Due dates, at-risk markers, and a "4 of 9 done" progress line for the day
  • Repeating tasks: every day, every working day, weekly, or monthly
  • Overlap warnings when timed tasks clash
  • A close-out reminder, quiet hours, eye-break reminders and a daily time budget
  • Stats: tasks dropped without a decision, your closeout streak, deadlines met, and carry-over rate per company
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive, and two-way Google Calendar sync
  • Optional AI recommendations at closeout, using your own Gemini or OpenRouter key
  • Works offline, installs like an app, and supports light and dark themes

Where your data lives

Your tasks are stored in your browser on your device. Taskuda has no server or database that holds your tasks, and no account to sign up for. If you connect Google Drive, backups go to a hidden app folder in your own Drive that other apps can't see. Your AI key and Jira token are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they're saved, including in backups; task content in a backup is protected by your Google account. AI recommendations are only sent to the provider you choose, after you confirm what will be sent.

Frequently asked questions

Is Taskuda free?

Yes. Taskuda is free and has no paid plans or accounts. The only possible cost is AI recommendations, which use your own Gemini or OpenRouter key, so any usage cost is between you and that provider. Everything else works without an AI key.

Where does Taskuda store my tasks?

In your browser, on your device. There is no Taskuda server or database that holds your tasks. If you connect Google Drive, Taskuda also keeps backups in a hidden app folder in your own Drive. Your AI key and Jira token are encrypted in those backups; task content is protected by your Google account.

Does Taskuda work offline?

Yes. After the first visit, you can create, edit and complete tasks, close out your day and copy your report with no connection. Drive backup, Calendar sync and AI recommendations need a connection.

Who is Taskuda for?

People who work for more than one employer or client at the same time, such as freelancers, contractors and people with two part-time jobs, especially if they send end-of-day reports to more than one manager.

Can I install Taskuda on my phone or computer?

Yes. Taskuda is a progressive web app. In Chrome or Edge, choose Install from the address bar or from Settings, About in Taskuda. On iPhone, open it in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen.

What happens to tasks I don't finish?

At the end of the day, the closeout asks you to decide what happens to every unfinished task: carry it over to your next working day, or delete it. Nothing is dropped without a decision, and tasks carried over several times are flagged as stuck.
