# Pricing — Taskuda

## Free (the only plan)

- Price: $0. No paid plans, trials or accounts.
- Limits: none set by Taskuda. Data is stored in your browser, so storage depends on your device.
- Features: every feature, including sticky-note planning by company, due dates and at-risk markers, repeating tasks, the end-of-day closeout and report, reminders, offline use, Google Drive backup and Google Calendar sync.

## What can cost money (billed by others, not Taskuda)

- AI recommendations at closeout: optional. They use your own Gemini or OpenRouter API key, so any usage cost is between you and that provider. Setting a spending limit with your provider is recommended.
- Google Drive storage: backups use your own Drive quota. They are small JSON files.
